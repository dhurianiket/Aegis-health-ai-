1. Multimodal Medical OCR (Gemini 2.5)

Extracts analytes, reference intervals, units, and flags from multi-page diagnostic lab PDFs and camera images via schema-constrained Gemini 2.5 Flash & Pro calls.

2. Longitudinal Biomarker Trajectories

Harmonizes historical lab values across Indian diagnostic chains (Thyrocare, Dr. Lal PathLabs, Metropolis, SRL) into multi-year velocity charts and composite health scores.

3. Physician SBAR Handoff Engine

Synthesizes Situation, Background, Assessment, and Recommendation (SBAR) briefs and specialist correlation matrices so doctors can review 5 years of history in 30 seconds.
