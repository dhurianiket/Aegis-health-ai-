A Note From Our Founder

"Health Data belongs to Indian Families, Not the Systems That Output It."

I founded Aegis Health AI because of a constant frustration: the lab reports we pay for are written for clinicians, not the anxious patients holding them. In Dombivli, Maharashtra, my family and neighbors would spend hours searching online for basic lipid or blood sugar explanations, only to face confusion or dread.

Leveraging my technical background in electronics and telecommunication engineering, along with an absolute obsession with designing secure generative systems, I built Aegis as a solo developer. My mission is to give you a patient-centric shield—a clear, reliable translator that enables you to step into the clinic with agency, prepared with the perfect questions to make your doctor visits 10x more valuable.

Aniket Rajesh Dhuri
AI Systems Builder & Founder, Aegis Health AI
