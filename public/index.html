My Personal Commitment

"Medical Reports Shouldn't Be a Source of Panic."

I built Aegis Health AI because of a problem close to my home. Like most Indian families, my parents and uncles would bring home blood tests or blood sugar reports, and we would gather around our kitchen table looking at a confusing grid of medical terms we didn't understand.

Searching the internet for these terms often led us to scary, dramatic pages that made simple out-of-bounds numbers feel like emergencies. I realized that our health information belongs to us, yet standard reports are written for computers and experts, not the families holding them.

As an electronics and telecommunication engineer and independent developer living in Dombivli, Maharashtra, I put my technical skills to work to create a friendly, safe translator. My mission is to give Indian homes a calm, supportive companion that helps you understand your reports and step into doctor visits with total confidence.

Aniket Rajesh Dhuri
Creator & Independent Developer, Aegis Health AI
