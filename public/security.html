Data Protection

Your Health Data. Fully Yours.

Aegis is built from the ground up to keep your personal health information private, safe, and completely under your control.

How We Keep Your Information Safe

Simple, robust protections built to give you and your family ultimate peace of mind

🔒

Private Folder for Every Family

Every lab report you upload and its parsed details are stored inside your own private, secure folder. No one else can see or access your family's personal files.

🛡️

Shielded Connections

Aegis connects to our AI translation backend using safe, hidden servers. We never expose raw API keys or credentials in the browser, keeping all communication lines highly secure.

📋

We Only Read What is Needed

Our platform is made to look only for standard blood and biomarker values. It ignores other private texts on your documents, processing only the essential metrics needed to explain your report.

Regulatory Commitment

Aligned with Indian Privacy Laws

Aegis is built in full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. We make sure you have complete authority over your information through simple choices:

  • Active Opt-In Consent: We will never read or process any file until you read and click our clear opt-in consent box.
  • Only What's Necessary: We collect only the bare indicators required to build your personal dashboards and trace your history over time. We don't ask for unneeded personal data.
  • Your Right to Ask and Erase: You have the absolute right to see your histories, request your data logs, or delete everything within seconds.

⚠️ OUR PLEDGE TO YOU

What We NEVER Do

To earn your trust and keep your family's confidence, we follow four absolute rules:

❌ We never sell or share your health data

We do not share, lease, trade, or sell your health metrics to insurance companies, advertising agencies, or pharmaceutical businesses.

❌ We never display advertisement banners

No annoying pop-up ads, target campaigns, or marketing tracking scripts infect your dashboard or clean reading workspace.

❌ We never use your data to train AI models

Your personal diagnostic values and uploaded report files are never used to train or improve external machine-learning models.

❌ We never keep files longer than they are needed

We do not keep permanent records for inactive accounts. You can wipe your data instantly, and we auto-delete temporary files quickly.

Delete Your Records Instantly

Your data is yours to keep, and yours to delete. We make it simple and direct to completely erase your footprint at any time.

Option 1: Self-Service Dashboard Deletion

  1. Open your Settings dashboard tab inside the application.
  2. Scroll down to the Data Controls and Privacy section.
  3. Click the Delete My Profile and Data button.
  4. Confirm your selection. The database will instantly execute a full system purge, permanently wiping out your profile, uploaded documents, chat history, medication trackers, and timeline trends. This holds zero backups.

Option 2: Email Deletion Request

  • Send an email to: support@aegishealthai.co.in
  • Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request
  • We will manually verify and process your permanent deletion request within 72 hours.
