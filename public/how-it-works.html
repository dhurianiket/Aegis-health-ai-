Our Process

How Aegis Health AI Works

Simply upload your clinical lab report, get a clear and friendly explanation in plain language, and prepare for your next doctor visit with confidence.

Four Simple Steps to Clarity

We guide you through your reports with total clarity, safety, and respect for your privacy

Step 01

Upload your report securely

Simply select or drag-and-drop your lab report PDF or image file into our platform. Your upload is fully protected under secure private encryption.

Step 02

Aegis reads the values

Our smart platform identifies standard lab indicators (like blood sugar patterns, red blood cells, or cholesterol metrics) and double-checks them against standard healthy ranges.

Step 03

Get explanation in plain language

Our friendly ChatCoach breaks down what each abbreviation means, translating clinical jargon into simple, comforting, and easily understandable terms for your family.

Step 04

Share a doctor-ready summary

Easily download a structured, professional summary of your key insights and trends. Take it directly to your doctor to have a much more meaningful and focused conversation.

Supported Report Types

We understand the standard primary care diagnostic tests ordered across India

🩸 CBC (Complete Blood Count)

Checks your active blood health, including your red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets.

🧪 LFT (Liver Function Tests)

Monitors how well your liver is filtering waste and maintaining chemical balances.

💧 KFT (Kidney Function Tests)

Measures how efficiently your kidneys are cleansing and balances essential electrolytes.

🧬 Lipid Profile (Cholesterol)

Analyzes your cholesterol levels, including healthy HDL and LDL trends, to review heart health.

🦋 Thyroid Tests

Checks your T3, T4, and TSH levels to ensure your body's energy regulation is balanced.

🍬 HbA1c & Diabetes Panel

Evaluates average blood sugar numbers over the last 90 days alongside fasting and post-meal sugar levels.

☀️ Vitamin D & B12 Levels

Tracks these vital essential nutrients, especially important for traditional Indian diets.

⚠️ SAFETY BOUNDARIES

What Aegis Health AI Does Not Do

We believe in absolute safety first. Aegis is built strictly for general health education, not medical treatment:

  • Aegis does not diagnose disease: We explain what your biomarker numbers mean, but we will never give a clinical diagnosis or try to name an illness.
  • Aegis does not replace your doctor: We do not offer telemedicine or make decisions for you. Always let a real doctor make the final decisions about your health care.
  • Aegis does not give treatment advice: You will never receive medicine prescriptions, chemical dosages, or direct treatment recommendations here.
  • Aegis does not keep your files: All uploaded files and analysis records are securely stored or automatically permanently deleted within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Plain answers to common questions about your reports, privacy, and safety

Is my data safe with Aegis?

Yes, completely. Aegis is built with a secure, user-locked privacy setup (using Firebase security). Your uploads are saved only in your isolated private folder, which is completely hidden from third parties. We never sell your health details, and you can delete your files instantly at any time.

Does Aegis replace my doctor?

No. Aegis does not replace your family doctor or specialist. Our goal is to make you feel more confident and prepared for your doctor appointments by explaining technical terms before your visit. You should always trust your physician for clinical diagnosis and treatment plans.

What reports can I upload?

Aegis supports standard clinical lab reports commonly ordered in India. This includes Complete Blood Count (CBC), Liver Function (LFT), Kidney Function (KFT), Lipid Profile, Thyroid, Vitamin D3, Vitamin B12, and HbA1c panels.

Is Aegis free to use?

Yes. We keep Aegis accessible for Indian families to translate and understand standard report values without subscription walls, helping everyone build better health literacy at home.

What is ChatCoach?

ChatCoach is our secure, friendly conversational tool. It lets you ask questions about your report in simple language (including Hinglish, Hindi, Marathi, or Gujarati) and explains complicated blood markers calmly without causing panic.

How does trend tracking work?

If you upload reports chronologically over time, Aegis connects the data points to show you if your levels are improving, stable, or rising. Seeing your health trends visually plotted on a timeline helps you and your doctor understand if your dietary or lifestyle adjustments are working.

What is an SBAR summary?

SBAR is a standardized, clean format (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation) that doctors and clinics use to communicate with each other. Aegis generates this clean summary of your health reports so you can show it to your doctor, saving them time and making your consult more efficient.

Is Aegis compliant with Indian data laws?

Yes. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. You are in complete control of your data, and we support immediate, permanent account deletion cascades with a single click.
