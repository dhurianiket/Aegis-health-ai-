Our Passion

We Built Aegis Because Medical Reports Shouldn't Be a Mystery

Helping Indian families understand their clinical lab reports in simple, patient-friendly language.

My Story

Hello, I am Aniket Rajesh Dhuri. I am a solo web developer, an AI generalist, and the creator of Aegis Health AI.

My journey did not begin in a corporate office or a sterile lab. It started in my home in Dombivli West, Maharashtra, India. I have always loved software, technology, and building visual experiences. In 2018, I completed my diploma in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering. Over the years, I worked in digital marketing and film production—even editing Bhojpuri feature films, which taught me how to tell engaging stories and build things that connect with real people. Today, I write code as a "vibe coder," focusing on making complex technology simple and helpful for everyday lives.

But when I wasn't coding, I was simply a son and nephew living in a typical Indian household. Every few months, my family members or neighbors would bring home medical lab reports. We would gather around our kitchen table, staring at a confusing spreadsheet of numbers and acronyms like HbA1c, ALT, or MCV. We had paid for these tests, yet the reports felt like they weren't written for us at all.

Like most families, we would immediately search the internet for answers. Web searches often showed scary, dramatic results, suggesting that a slightly high or low level was a major health emergency. Seeing my family get anxious over simple numbers was hard. I realized there had to be a better, gentler way to understand health. Health reports should bring clarity, not panic.

So, sitting at my desk in Dombivli—with my tabla in the corner and my acoustic guitar on the wall—I decided to build a solution. I designed Aegis Health AI to act as a friendly translator for your lab results.

I built this tool because I believe your health information belongs to you and your family. Aegis is here to give you simple, reliable context so you can take care of your loved ones, ask your doctor better questions, and feel completely in control of your journey.

Our Mission

Why Aegis Exists

Aegis exists to help real people understand what their blood tests and medical reports actually mean. We believe that health literacy is a fundamental right. No Indian family should have to search the internet in panic or wait in confusion just to understand a standard lab check-up.

Our goal is to give you clear, reassuring, and safe context so you can have more productive visits with your doctor and get the best guidance for your family.

01

Patient-First Clarity

We translate complicated medical terms and abbreviations into simple, warm, everyday language. Our goal is to make your reports feel welcoming and comforting, never scary.

02

Absolute Privacy

Your health data is entirely yours. We do not Sell your information or keep permanent records of your uploads. Your files are saved inside secure, private, user-locked accounts (users/{userId}) that you can delete instantly at any time.

03

Intelligent Context

Instead of just defining a dictionary word, we connect the dots. Aegis helps you see how different numbers relate to your daily habits and lifestyle so you get a helpful, clear overview.

04

Accessible to Every Family

We design Aegis to be simple and easy for parents, grandparents, and children alike to navigate. It is fast to load, easy to read, and works smoothly on any basic mobile phone.
